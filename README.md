
# BranchZero 🌱

**Every tech career starts somewhere.**

## About BranchZero

BranchZero is an in-development web platform designed to help college freshmen and sophomores discover, track, and pursue their first internship opportunities across technology-related fields.

Finding a first tech internship can be challenging for early-year students. Many opportunities target juniors and seniors, making it difficult for freshmen and sophomores to identify internships they may qualify for.

BranchZero aims to simplify this process by helping students discover relevant opportunities, identify eligibility requirements, and manage their applications in one place.

## Our Mission

Help college freshmen and sophomores find and land their first technology internship earlier in their academic journey.

## Who Is BranchZero For?

- College freshmen and sophomores
- Students searching for their first tech internship
- Students with little or no professional tech experience
- Students exploring software engineering, cybersecurity, data science, IT, and other technology-related fields

## Planned Features

- **Internship Discovery:** Find technology internships relevant to early-year college students.
- **Eligibility Filters:** Identify opportunities based on academic standing, graduation year, and application requirements.
- **Application Tracker:** Track internship applications and their current statuses.
- **Internship Deadlines:** Keep track of application deadlines and important dates.
- **Opportunity Alerts:** Receive updates about newly available internship opportunities.
- **Student Dashboard:** Manage discovered opportunities and submitted applications in one place.
